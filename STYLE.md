# Al's KC Cleaning — style notes

Taken from the live als-cleaning.com theme (Clenq), not from a new palette.

| Role | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Navy | `#043e74` | Headings, body text, utility bar, footer, process band |
| Blue | `#0860b2` | Links, focus rings |
| Bright blue | `#298be7` | Header stripe and the scroll progress bar |
| Yellow | `#fff203` | Primary buttons, eyebrow ticks, accents on navy |
| Ice | `#f3f8fc` | Alternating section background |
| White | `#ffffff` | Page background and cards |
| Muted | `#3d5270` | Secondary text on light backgrounds |

Buttons are pills. The primary button is yellow with navy text. The secondary button is a navy outline. On a phone, the bottom bar is navy for Call and yellow for Book.

Type is Outfit for headings and Inter for body, the same families as the current site.

Motion: a blue progress bar, a header shadow after you scroll, sections that rise into view, a slow hero photo shift, cards that lift on hover, and a yellow line that fills beside the three steps on small screens. The story under Why Al's is a sticky stage: scrolling crossfades the founder, the LEAD Academy, University Academy, and Wonderscope photos, then the two clips. The results section pins and slides that same film sideways on wide screens. On a phone the film swipes. All of that turns off when the visitor prefers reduced motion, and the chapters stack instead.
